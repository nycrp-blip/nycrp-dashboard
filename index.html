Who We Are

Welcome to South London Roleplay

South London Roleplay is an ER:LC community focused on bringing players together through engaging roleplay, community interaction and organised experiences.

Whether you enjoy law enforcement roleplay, emergency services, civilian activities or simply meeting other members, SLRP provides a place to get involved.

Our aim is to build a welcoming community where members can participate, develop their roleplay and enjoy their time with others.
